## 👋 About 1 in 8 Billion

**1 in 8 Billion** asks a simple question: *if you were born right now, who would you be?* The game starts you in a random country and family, weighted by real-world statistics, then lets you make choices through school, work, relationships and later life.

You can play in today's world, travel to the past or imagine the future, and watch four parallel lives unfold side by side. Lives that are lived to the end can be saved to your Life Album or shared — anonymously, with just a few words from the person who lived them.

It runs in any browser on phone or computer, with no download, and every game feature is free after signing in. The country odds, life expectancy and events are based on public data; the methodology page explains exactly what is data and what is game design.

## ✨ Features

- **🌍 Born anywhere** — your country is drawn by annual births ("Babies born today") or by population share ("The 8 billion alive").
- **⏳ Every era** — be born into the past, today's world or a possible future.
- **🔀 Parallel lives** — compare how four lives unfold from the same starting second.
- **🎯 Real choices** — school, work, relationships, family and later life, with clear limits on what you can and can't choose.
- **📚 Life Album & Memorial** — keep finished lives, and read lives shared by other players.
- **⏱️ Your pace** — pick 30, 60 or 90 minutes per life and adjust speed as you play.
- **🫶 Gentle options** — tone down difficult moments and turn on gentle music; graphic scenes are never depicted.
- **📅 Daily theme** — each day new lives meet a themed event, such as road safety.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Sign in** and choose a world: the past, today or the future.
2. **Pick how your country is chosen** and how long a life should take.
3. **Be born** — then make choices through each stage of life.
4. **Save the life** to your album, share it, or start another one.

## 💡 Why People Play

- **See the world differently** — experience how much your birthplace shapes your chances.
- **Classroom & discussion** — a gentle way to talk about inequality, health and demographics.
- **Short, replayable sessions** — every life is different.

### 👋 关于 1 in 8 Billion

**1 in 8 Billion（八十亿分之一）** 只问一个问题：*如果你此刻出生，你会是谁？* 游戏按真实统计数据加权，把你随机分配到某个国家和家庭，再让你在学业、工作、感情和晚年中做出选择。

你可以出生在今天的世界，也可以回到过去或想象未来，还能同时观察四段平行人生。走完的人生可以存入「人生相册」或匿名分享，只留下这个人想说的几句话。

手机电脑浏览器即可游玩，无需下载；登录后所有功能免费。国家概率、预期寿命等基于公开数据，「方法说明」页清楚列出哪些是数据、哪些是游戏设计。

### ✨ 功能特点

- **🌍 生在世界任何地方**：按每年出生人数（今天出生的婴儿）或人口占比（活着的 80 亿人）抽取国家。
- **⏳ 任意时代**：出生在过去、今天或可能的未来。
- **🔀 平行人生**：对比同一秒出生的四段人生如何展开。
- **🎯 真实的选择**：学业、工作、感情、家庭、晚年，并说明哪些能选、哪些不能选。
- **📚 人生相册与纪念馆**：保存走完的人生，阅读其他玩家分享的人生。
- **⏱️ 自定节奏**：每段人生 30、60 或 90 分钟，游戏中可调速度。
- **🫶 温和模式**：可弱化沉重情节、开启舒缓音乐，不会出现血腥画面。
- **📅 每日主题**：每天的新人生都会遇到一个主题事件，例如交通安全。

### 🎮 玩法

1. **登录**并选择世界：过去、今天或未来。
2. **选择国家抽取方式**和每段人生的时长。
3. **出生**，然后在人生各阶段做出选择。
4. **保存人生**到相册、分享，或再开一局。

### 💡 为什么值得玩

- **换个角度看世界**：亲身感受出生地对命运的影响。
- **课堂与讨论**：温和地讨论不平等、健康和人口话题。
- **短局可重玩**：每一段人生都不一样。
